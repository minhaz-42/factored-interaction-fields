## 5. Problems found today (all fixed, but read before resuming)

1. **False completion marker (caused real damage).** In
   `python ... | grep | tee log && echo "pipeline done"`, a killed Python still
   returns success because the pipeline's status comes from `tee`. This wrote a
   premature "pipeline done", which started experiments on a 10% cache.
   *Fixed:* `set -o pipefail` in launchers, and `cache_features.py` now prints a
   unique `CACHE_COMPLETE` marker of its own.
   **Two fold-B runs trained on the partial cache were deleted as void.**

2. **Three models loaded at once caused swap-thrash.** Two cache processes plus a
   trainer put processes into uninterruptible wait and dropped throughput from about
   12 img/s to 5. This matches the known 16 GB single-model rule for this machine.
   *Rule going forward: exactly one GPU process at a time.*

3. **Batch size 32 is slower than 16** for caching on this GPU (6.6 vs 9.1 img/s).
   Keep `--batch 16`.

4. **Lazy positional embedding bug** in the model: it was created inside `forward`,
   so it was missing from the optimizer and from checkpoints. *Fixed* (now a real
   parameter sized from the token grid). Any checkpoint made before this fix is void.

5. **Misleading cache rate.** The logged rate counted skipped scenes. *Fixed* to
   report only newly processed images.

## 8. The research question, for context when resuming

The interaction field is a deterministic function of three things: the hand joints,
the object's 6-DoF pose, and a known mesh. All three are supervised in training and
the object category is given at test time, yet every published solution regresses the
126 numbers directly. FIF predicts the arguments and applies the function through a
differentiable soft nearest-vertex operator, then fuses that with a direct head by
predicted precision.

Falsification criterion, agreed before any coding: if at a matched frozen backbone the
hybrid does not beat the direct head on ADE, **and** the geometric head is not better
on far-field or occluded strata, **and** transfer is not improved, the hypothesis is
rejected. The decomposition and observability analysis remain publishable either way.

One day-one hypothesis is already refuted by the data: unobservable targets are *not*
a major error source, since the object is out of view in only 1.3% of labelled frames.
The error budget must instead be sought in the heavy magnitude tail (30.2% of joints
exceed 60 mm) and in the 24% of joints that are occluded while in contact.
